A native AI conversation, inside your Mac app.

  • Version {{version}}
  • Swift package
  • macOS 14 or later

AgentKit puts a full AI conversation inside your macOS app, powered by the Claude Code or Codex CLI already installed on the Mac. It is the conversation from Tab, with its interface, runtime and saved sessions, made into a package any app can use.

Built for AI-first apps

Your app describes what it can do, and the agent does it through your own tools. AgentKit brings the conversation, streaming, model selection, tool results and sessions, and the same tools work with both providers.

Everything a conversation needs

Streaming replies with Markdown, code, tables, math and diagrams. A composer with attachments, model and reasoning choices, approvals, questions and a prompt queue. Conversations and drafts are saved, so people pick up where they left off after a relaunch.

Your app stays in charge

When your app hands the agent its own tools, the provider's built-in tools are switched off or kept read-only. Your app checks every request and makes each change through its own editing and undo. When it quits, AgentKit saves the conversation and stops the agent.

Runs on what is already there

AgentKit talks to the Claude Code or Codex CLI installed on the Mac, with the account people already use. It bundles no model and manages no accounts, and its example app runs an offline demo with no account at all.

At work in Tab and Filmatte

Tab runs its agent panes on AgentKit, and Filmatte, a native video editor, lets an agent add text and cut the timeline through the editor's own tools. Both connect the same package in different ways, as the docs show.
